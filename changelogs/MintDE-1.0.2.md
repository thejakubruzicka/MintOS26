# MintDE 1.0.2

- Per-app PNG icon assets, linked to bounded RGBA bitmap rendering.
- Settings dropdowns and fixed content bounds to prevent panel spill.
- Refined cursor shape, cursor damage redraw, and dock repaint behavior.
- Translucent panel sampling and specular edges for a more readable glass effect.
- Terminal adds mount, PCI, driver, storage-I/O and recent-log commands.
