Mozilla CA certificate store
============================

assets/certs/cacert.pem is the Mozilla CA certificate list as extracted by the
curl project (https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html) from Mozilla's certdata.txt
(NSS). kernel/user/crypto/roots_data.c is generated from it by
tools/make_roots.py and contains only each root's subject name and public key.

The bundle header records the Mozilla data date and the SHA-256 of the source
certdata.txt. To refresh it, download a new cacert.pem from
https://curl.se/ca/cacert.pem into assets/certs/ and run tools/make_roots.py,
which verifies every certificate against its SHA-256 fingerprint.
